## How does it work?

FIVO does not ask you to rate a hundred films out of ten. It asks what
matters: **the works that stayed with you**. Search for a series, a movie or
a book, say whether you have seen and loved it, whether it is not for you, or
whether you want to see it — that is all.

From those few moves, FIVO crosses two kinds of knowledge: **the community** —
thousands of shared top fives, from people whose taste is close to yours —
and the **cultural fingerprint** of each work, a signature in six dimensions
that measures how much two stories really resemble each other, beyond the
genre on the label.

The result: suggestions that fit, and that are explained — you always know
*why* a work is being offered to you.
